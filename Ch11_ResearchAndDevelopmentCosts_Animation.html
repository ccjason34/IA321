Research and Development Costs

Kieso, Weygandt & Warfield · Intermediate Accounting 18th Ed. · Chapter 11 · Intangible Assets

R&D Is Usually Expensed Immediately
The accounting issue is not whether the project might become valuable. The issue is whether the cost creates a reliably measurable asset today.
Core U.S. GAAP rule: research and development costs are generally expensed as incurred. Student trap: do not capitalize internal R&D just because management expects the project to succeed.
Research and Development
Searching for new knowledge, designing prototypes, testing alternatives, and developing a new product before commercial production.
Usually Dr R&D Expense
Not R&D
Routine engineering, quality control, advertising, training, and production after the product is ready for sale are not R&D.
Use the regular account
Best first question: is the cost part of discovering or developing a new product/process, or is it ordinary production, selling, training, or quality control?
