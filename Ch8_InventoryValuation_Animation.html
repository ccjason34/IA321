Lower of Cost or Market (LCM)
Unlike LCNRV, "market" here is more nuanced — it is the middle of three values: Replacement Cost, a Ceiling, and a Floor.
Ready to start: Click Step 1: Choose Designated Market. For each item we will first choose the middle number out of Replacement Cost, Ceiling, and Floor. Then Step 2 compares that market with Cost and chooses the lower number.
The Designated Market Rule:
Market = Replacement Cost, but bounded:
  • Ceiling = Net Realizable Value (Selling price − Completion & selling costs)
  • Floor = NRV − Normal Profit Margin
Then: compare Cost with the Designated Market and use whichever amount is lower.

LCM vs LCNRV — when to use which? Under current U.S. GAAP, LCM applies only to inventory measured using LIFO or the Retail Inventory Method. All other inventory (FIFO, weighted average) uses the simpler LCNRV rule shown in the previous tab. Under IFRS, LCNRV is always used.
