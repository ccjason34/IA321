The Scenario: A Store With Thousands of Items

Boc Apparel sells many styles and sizes. Counting every item at cost during the year is slow, but the store already tracks goods at retail prices. The retail inventory method estimates ending inventory by converting ending inventory at retail back to estimated cost.

Conventional Retail Method - In Three Moves
The conventional retail inventory method estimates ending inventory at a conservative amount by using a cost-to-retail ratio that excludes net markdowns.
Memory hook
RATIO BEFORE markdowns · ENDING RETAIL AFTER markdowns
The ratio uses the old retail subtotal; the inventory left on hand uses the marked-down retail amount.

Why this is LCM-like: A markdown signals goods are not worth full retail. Including the markdown in the ratio would make the ratio higher and inventory higher; excluding it keeps the ratio and carrying value lower.

What Goes Into the Ratio?

Markups are included. Markdowns are excluded.

Retail itemConventional ratio treatment
Beginning inventory at retailInclude
Net purchases at retailInclude
Net markupsInclude
Net markdownsExclude from ratio

Why Exclude Markdowns?

This is the conventional lower-of-cost-or-market effect.

Effect: Conventional uses a larger denominator, so the ratio is lower. That lower ratio keeps the final inventory estimate conservative.
Test cue: If the question says conventional retail method, do not subtract net markdowns when computing the cost-to-retail ratio.
