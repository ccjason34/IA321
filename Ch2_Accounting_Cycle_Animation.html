What Still Needs Adjustment — 3 Conditions
① Supplies — Deferral
Pioneer purchased $2,500 of advertising supplies on Oct 5. At Oct 31, a physical count shows only $1,000 remains on hand. That means $1,500 was used up during October — but no Supplies Expense has been recorded yet. The Supplies account is overstated by $1,500.
② Interest — Accrual
On Oct 1, Pioneer borrowed $5,000 on a 12% annual note payable. By Oct 31, one full month of interest has built up: $5,000 × 12% ÷ 12 months = $50. This $50 of interest expense has been incurred but not yet paid or recorded. Both Interest Expense and Interest Payable are missing from the trial balance entirely.
③ Unearned Revenue — Deferral
On Oct 15, Pioneer received $12,000 cash from Knox Co. as an advance for future advertising services. By Oct 31, Pioneer has performed $4,000 worth of those services. The entire $12,000 still sits as a liability (Unearned Service Revenue) — it needs to be reduced by $4,000 and recognized as earned revenue.

Step 5 of 9

Adjusting Entries

Three entries bring accounts up to date so revenue and expenses fall in the right period. No cash changes hands — these are internal corrections.

🔄 Deferrals

Cash was paid/received before the cost expired or revenue was earned. Shift from balance sheet → income statement.

📈 Accruals

Revenue earned or expense incurred but cash has not yet been exchanged. Add receivable or payable + income statement effect.
