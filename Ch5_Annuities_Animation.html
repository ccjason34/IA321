One Payment Shift Changes the Value
An annuity is a series of equal payments or receipts, called rents, at equal time intervals. The key distinction is timing: ordinary annuity payments happen at the end of each period; annuity due payments happen at the beginning of each period.
Core idea: An annuity due is the same stream of payments shifted one period earlier. Because each payment happens sooner, it earns one extra period of interest for future value and is discounted one fewer period for present value.
Ordinary Annuity
Payment timing: End of each period.

Annuity Due

Present Value: Which Annuity Is Worth More Today?
For present value, earlier payments are discounted for fewer periods. That is why an annuity due has a larger present value than an ordinary annuity with the same payment, rate, and number of payments.
