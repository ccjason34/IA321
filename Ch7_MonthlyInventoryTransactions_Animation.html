Question 1
Which costs go to COGS?
FIFO sends oldest costs first. LIFO sends newest costs first.
Question 2
When do we apply the cost flow rule?
Periodic waits until month-end. Perpetual applies the rule at each sale date.

Periodic: collect the sales, then cost them once
Under periodic inventory, sales during the month do not remove cost layers one by one. We collect the total units sold first, then apply FIFO or LIFO once at month-end.
Periodic memory hook: sales go into a bucket during the month. The cost flow rule is applied only after we know total units sold and ending units.

Perpetual: each sale removes inventory immediately
Under perpetual inventory, every sale records COGS on that sale date. That timing is the key reason LIFO perpetual can differ from LIFO periodic.
Perpetual memory hook: each sale can only use layers that already exist on that date. On Jan. 4, the $6 and $7 layers are future purchases, so LIFO cannot use them yet.
